About TYPEGLOW

TYPEGLOW is a typing test application designed to help you improve your typing speed and accuracy. It offers various modes and customizable durations to suit your practice needs.

We believe that practicing regularly can significantly enhance your typing skills. TYPEGLOW provides a platform for consistent practice and progress tracking.
